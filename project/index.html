Travel Snapshot

Why travelers choose Costa Rica

  • Easy mix of beach, rainforest, wildlife, and mountain destinations.
  • Great choice for families, nature lovers, and first-time international travelers.
  • Compact geography makes it possible to enjoy multiple regions in one trip.

Local Tip

Today’s planning idea

Pack light layers so you can move easily between warm coastlines and cooler mountain areas.

Beach mornings, rainforest afternoons

Many itineraries combine relaxed coastal time with inland activities such as hanging bridges, hot springs, and wildlife watching.

Cultural stops add balance

A stronger trip plan includes food, local towns, and meaningful landmarks in addition to outdoor activities.
