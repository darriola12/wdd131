Site Name

Explore Costa Rica

I chose this name because it is simple, clear, and directly connected to the purpose of the site. It presents the project as a travel guide for people who want to discover destinations, activities, and planning information related to Costa Rica.

Optional domain availability: explorecostaricaguide.com

Site Purpose

The site will provide a helpful guide for visitors who want to learn about Costa Rica before planning a trip. It will include featured destinations, activity ideas, travel tips, and contact information for requesting more details or recommendations.

Scenarios

  • Which destinations in Costa Rica are best for beaches, mountains, or rainforest adventures?
  • What should I pack and prepare for before visiting Costa Rica?
  • Where can I find recommended activities for families or first-time visitors?
